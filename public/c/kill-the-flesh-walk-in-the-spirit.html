Holds

1

Thought capture

Destructive thought appears — capture it.

  • Stop.
  • Name it.
  • Do not feed it.
  • Submit it to Christ.
Prayer

“No. I release this to You, Lord.”

2 Corinthians 10:5 Colossians 3:2

2

Refuse the case-hunt

The mind hunts evidence. Gaps become “proof.” Peace rarely follows.

  • Name it: “This is a case-hunt.”
  • Refuse every lead.
  • Do not turn open questions into a case right now.
Prayer

“I refuse to live as the permanent investigator. I hand this uncertainty to You.”

2 Corinthians 10:5 Philippians 4:8 Romans 12:2

3

Refuse condemnation

The accuser’s voice: self-hate loops, sticky words of others, judgment, fiery darts. Conviction is specific and leads to life. Condemnation freezes.

  • Name it: “This is condemnation — not the Holy Spirit.”
  • Own what is real once.
  • Receive cleansing. Take one free step.
Prayer

“There is no condemnation in Christ Jesus. I refuse the accuser’s voice. Cleanse me and raise me.”

Romans 8:1 Revelation 12:10 1 John 1:9 2 Corinthians 7:10

4

Fight fear

Fear is fought with power, love, and a sound mind.

  • Name the fear.
  • Refuse to agree with it.
  • Hand the outcome to God.
Prayer

“God has not given me a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and a sound mind. I hand this to You.”

2 Timothy 1:7 Ephesians 6:16 Philippians 4:6–7

5

Refuse the flesh

No name-calling, rage, control, monitoring, revenge, or settled bitterness.

  • Name the act.
  • Refuse it.
  • Return to obedience.
Prayer

“I refuse this. I will walk in Love.”

Galatians 5:19–21 Ephesians 4:29 Ephesians 4:31

6

Forgiveness

Forgiveness refuses the scorecard and bitterness.

  • Release the debt.
  • Stop rehearsing the offense.
  • Hand them and the injustice to God.
Prayer

“I forgive. I release this to You, Lord.”

Matthew 6:14–15 Ephesians 4:32 Colossians 3:13

7

Self-control

Guard eyes, thoughts, and body. Do not push for intimacy or force deep talks.

  • Stop the urge.
  • Do not chase self-gratification.
  • Submit the thought to Christ.
Prayer

“My body is the temple of the Holy Spirit. I choose self-control. Strengthen me.”

1 Corinthians 6:18–20 Galatians 5:22–23

8

Presence

You are whole under God. Love without disappearing or managing their feelings.

  • Do not over-function.
  • Be present where God has you.
  • Do not let their choices rule your peace.
Prayer

“Lord, this is where You have me. Help me be present where I am.”

Colossians 3:23 Galatians 6:4–5 Psalm 16:8

9

Honest assessment

You may not fully trust them. Do not pretend. Do not use distrust as a license for the flesh.

  • Admit it.
  • Do not overcommit.
  • Still refuse the flesh.
Prayer

“Lord, I do not trust them. Give me peace.”

Proverbs 4:23 Ephesians 4:15

10

Contact and boundaries

When the bond is unstable: low contact. Logistics only. Not to calm your pain or force reassurance.

  • Short and clear.
  • Kids, house, money, schedules — not the relationship.
  • If the urge is to manage pain, do not send.
Prayer

“I will not use contact to manage pain. Give me restraint.”

Matthew 5:37 Ecclesiastes 3:1

11

Do your part

You own thoughts, mouth, actions, obedience, standards. Their replies and plans are theirs.

  • Notice the drift.
  • Stop building stories.
  • Return to your part.
Prayer

“Lord, You know what they are doing. I give them to You.”

1 Thessalonians 4:11 Galatians 6:4–5 Matthew 7:3

12

Outside pressure

Old patterns and third parties return. Do not compete or spy.

  • Notice the pull.
  • Do your part.
  • Hand the situation to God.
Prayer

“Lord, I will not compete. I entrust this to You.”

Romans 12:17–19

13

Strength, not need

Do not reach out from anxiety. Do not measure worth by their tone.

  • Stop reaching from need.
  • Bring the raw sentence to the Lord.
  • Measure by obedience, not their response.
Prayer

“My peace is not dependent on them. I hand this to You.”

Psalm 46:10 Exodus 14:14 1 Peter 5:7

14

Trust over understanding

Trust the Lord. Do not lean on your own understanding.

  • Refuse the demand to figure it all out.
  • Hand unanswered questions to Him.
  • Do your part.
Prayer

“Lord, You know the plans You have for me. Help me trust You.”

Proverbs 3:5–6 Jeremiah 29:11

For men

Lead yourself first. Carry your own load. Kind, clear, and boundaried — not passive, not aggressive.

  • No one else steadies your peace for you.
  • Courage stays steady without forcing closeness.
  • Govern yourself; do not manage her.

1 Corinthians 16:13–14 Proverbs 25:28 Joshua 1:9 Ephesians 5:25

Success

Not whether they return or choose you. Staying in the Spirit — one decision at a time.

Do better. Be better. Trust God.

Galatians 6:9 James 1:12
