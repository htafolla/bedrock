# His Promises

> God is faithful. He will never leave you nor forsake you.

*Station · Hold first (Under fire → Prayer → Truth) · Bedrock · https://bedrock.rippel.ai/c/his-promises*

## Under fire

- Trade “what if they never…” for what He already promised.
- No one snatches you from His hand. Not even your shame.

## Prayer

Lord, You are faithful. Anchor me to Your word. Amen.

## Truth

Once you are in His hand, no one can snatch you out.

The work of God is this: to believe in the One He has sent.

Abide in Me, and I will abide in you.

He is faithful. His word is true. He never breaks His promises.
