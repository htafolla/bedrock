# Standard: Kill the Flesh. Walk in the Spirit.

> Name the flesh. Filter it. Kill it in three steps. Tools for this hour. Walk by the Spirit.

*Standard · Field card first · Hold first · Bedrock · https://bedrock.rippel.ai/c/kill-the-flesh-walk-in-the-spirit*

## Under fire

- Name it: fear — the flesh. Stop the thought. Capture it. Give it to Christ.
- Three filters: self-less? Protect? Honor God? If no — stop.
- Stop. Breathe. Choose the other path. Do your part. Success is Spirit, not outcomes.

## Prayer

Father, this is fear — this is the flesh. I take it captive to Christ. I refuse control, jealousy, impatience, and rage. Walk me by the Spirit. Amen.

## The standard

## Field card

Kill the acts of the flesh. Walk in the Spirit. This is the path — not a mood.

[Galatians 5:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A16&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:22–23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A22-23&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:24–25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A24-25&version=NIV)

### 1. Name it

Before you fight, name what is running.

- Surfaces: Thoughts · In-person (words, tone, body, messages).
- Root: Fear.
- Fear produces: Control · Jealousy · Impatience.
- Downstream: Fear → Control / Jealousy / Impatience → Rage → Bitterness · Habit · Immediate relief.

Say it plain: “This is fear. This is the flesh.” Do not dress it up as “just caring,” “just needing to know,” or “just honesty.”

[2 Timothy 1:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Timothy+1%3A7&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:19–21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A19-21&version=NIV) · [James 1:19–20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+1%3A19-20&version=NIV)

### 2. Three filters

Run the act (or the urge) through three questions. All three must pass.

- Is it self-less? (Love does not seek its own way.)
- Does it protect? (Body, soul, family, the weak — not ego, not image.)
- Does it honor God? (Would you do this with Christ in the room?)

If any answer is no → stop. Do not negotiate with the flesh.

[1 Corinthians 13:4–7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+13%3A4-7&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A16&version=NIV) · [Luke 9:23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+9%3A23&version=NIV)

### 3. Kill the flesh (3 steps)

- Name it — “This is fear. This is the flesh.”
- Capture it — Take the thought captive. Give it to Christ. Do not feed it.
- Stop. Breathe. Choose the other path — the next right act in the Spirit, not the old relief.

Prayer: “Father, I take every thought captive. This is flesh. I refuse it. I walk in the Spirit. Amen.”

[2 Corinthians 10:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+10%3A5&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:24–25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A24-25&version=NIV) · [Romans 8:13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8%3A13&version=NIV)

### 4. Tools in the moment

When the body is hot and the mind is loud — use steel tools, not more thinking.

- Exhale hard once — empty the panic breath.
- 3-2-1 grounding — 3 things you see, 2 you hear, 1 you feel. Back in the room.
- Default phrase: “My emotional state is my own.” Their choices do not own your peace.

Then return to step 3. Do your part. Their replies are theirs. Success is Spirit, not outcomes.

[James 1:19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+1%3A19&version=NIV) · [Proverbs 25:28](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+25%3A28&version=NIV) · [Galatians 6:4–5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+6%3A4-5&version=NIV)

## Holds

When the field card is not enough — same steel, more map. Use under fire first; open these when capacity returns.

### 1. Thought capture

Destructive thought appears — capture it. This is the first kill.

- Stop the thought.
- Name it: fear, case-hunt, accusation, lust, rage.
- Do not feed it.
- Give it to Christ.

Prayer: “No. I release this to You, Lord.”

[2 Corinthians 10:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+10%3A5&version=NIV) · [Colossians 3:2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Colossians+3%3A2&version=NIV)

### 2. Refuse building a case

Fear hunts evidence. Gaps become “proof.” Peace rarely follows.

- Name it: “I am building a case.”
- Refuse every lead.
- Do not turn open questions into a case right now.

Prayer: “I refuse to live as the detective of every gap. I hand this uncertainty to You.”

[2 Corinthians 10:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+10%3A5&version=NIV) · [Philippians 4:8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Philippians+4%3A8&version=NIV) · [Romans 12:2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+12%3A2&version=NIV)

### 3. Refuse condemnation

The accuser’s voice: self-hate loops, harsh words that stick, judgment, fiery darts. Conviction is specific and leads to life. Condemnation freezes.

- Name it: “This is condemnation — not the Holy Spirit.”
- Own what is real once.
- Receive cleansing. Take one free step in the Spirit now.

Prayer: “There is no condemnation in Christ Jesus. I refuse the accuser’s voice. Cleanse me and raise me.”

[Romans 8:1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8%3A1&version=NIV) · [Revelation 12:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+12%3A10&version=NIV) · [1 John 1:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+John+1%3A9&version=NIV) · [2 Corinthians 7:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+7%3A10&version=NIV)

### 4. Fight fear at the root

Fear is the root. Control, jealousy, and impatience are its fruit. Rage, bitterness, habit, and cheap relief are further downstream.

- Name the fear — not only the behavior.
- Refuse to agree with it.
- Hand the outcome to God. Power, love, sound mind.

Prayer: “God has not given me a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and a sound mind. I hand this to You.”

[2 Timothy 1:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Timothy+1%3A7&version=NIV) · [Ephesians 6:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+6%3A16&version=NIV) · [Philippians 4:6–7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Philippians+4%3A6-7&version=NIV)

### 5. Refuse the flesh acts

No name-calling, rage, control, monitoring, revenge, or settled bitterness. No jealousy dressed as love. No impatience dressed as urgency.

- Name the act.
- Run the three filters. If no — stop.
- Return to obedience: one free step.

Prayer: “I refuse this. I will walk in Love.”

[Galatians 5:19–21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A19-21&version=NIV) · [Ephesians 4:29](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+4%3A29&version=NIV) · [Ephesians 4:31](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+4%3A31&version=NIV) · [1 Corinthians 13:4–7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+13%3A4-7&version=NIV)

### 6. Forgiveness

Forgiveness refuses the scorecard and bitterness. It is not saying the harm was small.

- Release the debt.
- Stop rehearsing the offense.
- Hand them and the injustice to God.

Prayer: “I forgive. I release this to You, Lord.”

[Matthew 6:14–15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+6%3A14-15&version=NIV) · [Ephesians 4:32](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+4%3A32&version=NIV) · [Colossians 3:13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Colossians+3%3A13&version=NIV)

### 7. Self-control

A man without rule of his spirit is a city broken into. Guard eyes, mouth, body, and send button.

- Stop the urge.
- Exhale. Ground. Default phrase if needed.
- Submit the thought to Christ. One duty you own today.

Prayer: “My body is the temple of the Holy Spirit. I choose self-control. Strengthen me.”

[Proverbs 25:28](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+25%3A28&version=NIV) · [1 Corinthians 6:18–20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+6%3A18-20&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:22–23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A22-23&version=NIV) · [Titus 2:11–12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Titus+2%3A11-12&version=NIV)

### 8. Presence

You are whole under God. Love without vanishing or managing their feelings.

- Do not over-function.
- Be present where God has you.
- Do not let their choices rule your peace. My emotional state is my own.

Prayer: “Lord, this is where You have me. Help me be present where I am.”

[Colossians 3:23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Colossians+3%3A23&version=NIV) · [Galatians 6:4–5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+6%3A4-5&version=NIV) · [Psalm 16:8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+16%3A8&version=NIV)

### 9. Honest assessment

You may not fully trust them. Do not pretend. Do not use distrust as a license for the flesh.

- Admit it.
- Do not overcommit.
- Still refuse the flesh.

Prayer: “Lord, I do not trust them. Give me peace.”

[Proverbs 4:23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+4%3A23&version=NIV) · [Ephesians 4:15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+4%3A15&version=NIV)

### 10. Contact and boundaries

When the bond is unstable: low contact. Logistics only. Not to calm your pain or force reassurance.

- Short and clear.
- Kids, house, money, schedules — not the relationship.
- If the urge is to manage pain, do not send. Filters first.

Prayer: “I will not use contact to manage pain. Give me restraint.”

[Matthew 5:37](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+5%3A37&version=NIV) · [Ecclesiastes 3:1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ecclesiastes+3%3A1&version=NIV)

### 11. Do your part

You own thoughts, mouth, actions, obedience, standards. Their replies and plans are theirs.

- Notice the drift into control or case-hunt.
- Stop building stories.
- Return to your part. One free step.

Prayer: “Lord, You know what they are doing. I give them to You.”

[1 Thessalonians 4:11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Thessalonians+4%3A11&version=NIV) · [Galatians 6:4–5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+6%3A4-5&version=NIV) · [Matthew 7:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+7%3A3&version=NIV)

### 12. Outside pressure

Old patterns and third parties return. Do not compete or spy.

- Notice the pull.
- Do your part.
- Hand the situation to God.

Prayer: “Lord, I will not compete. I entrust this to You.”

[Romans 12:17–19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+12%3A17-19&version=NIV)

### 13. Strength, not need

Do not reach out from anxiety. Do not measure worth by their tone.

- Stop reaching from need.
- Bring the raw sentence to the Lord.
- Measure by obedience, not their response.

Prayer: “My peace is not dependent on them. I hand this to You.”

[Psalm 46:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm+46%3A10&version=NIV) · [Exodus 14:14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+14%3A14&version=NIV) · [1 Peter 5:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Peter+5%3A7&version=NIV)

### 14. Trust over understanding

Trust the Lord. Do not lean on your own understanding. Fear demands a full map; faith takes the next step.

- Refuse the demand to figure it all out.
- Hand unanswered questions to Him.
- Do your part.

Prayer: “Lord, You know the plans You have for me. Help me trust You.”

[Proverbs 3:5–6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+3%3A5-6&version=NIV) · [Jeremiah 29:11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jeremiah+29%3A11&version=NIV)

## For men

Lead yourself first. Carry your own load. Kind, clear, and boundaried — not passive, not aggressive. Govern the flesh before you speak.

- No one else steadies your peace for you. My emotional state is my own.
- Courage stays steady without forcing closeness.
- Govern yourself; do not manage her. Rule of spirit — not a city broken into.

[1 Corinthians 16:13–14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+16%3A13-14&version=NIV) · [Proverbs 25:28](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+25%3A28&version=NIV) · [Joshua 1:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Joshua+1%3A9&version=NIV) · [Ephesians 5:25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+5%3A25&version=NIV)
