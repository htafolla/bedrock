# Forgive as You Have Been Forgiven

> Forgive one another as God in Christ forgave you.

*Station · Hold first (Under fire → Prayer → Truth) · Bedrock · https://bedrock.rippel.ai/c/forgive-as-you-have-been-forgiven*

## Under fire

- Forgiveness is not saying it did not hurt. It is releasing vengeance to God.
- Unforgiveness chains you to the wound. Forgive and obey while grief remains.

## Prayer

Lord, as You forgave me, I release this debt. Amen.

## Truth

If you do not forgive others their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.

Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another.
