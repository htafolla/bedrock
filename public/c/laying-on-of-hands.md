# Laying on of Hands

> Hands laid in faith under the name of Jesus.

*Station · Hold first (Under fire → Prayer → Truth) · Bedrock · https://bedrock.rippel.ai/c/laying-on-of-hands*

## Under fire

- Hands under Jesus’ name are faith, not magic.
- Invite mature believers. Avoid show.
- If you are prayed for: receive. Do not perform.
