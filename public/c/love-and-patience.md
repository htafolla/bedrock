# Love and Patience

> True love is patient, kind, and keeps no record of wrongs.

*Station · Hold first (Under fire → Prayer → Truth) · Bedrock · https://bedrock.rippel.ai/c/love-and-patience*

## Under fire

- Love keeps no record of wrongs. Stop replaying the list of their failures.
- Patience is love under pressure. Slow the reply. Soften the tone. Protect, trust, hope, persevere.

## Prayer

Lord, I release the ledger of wrongs. Soften me to love and wait as You love. Amen.

## Truth

Love does not envy, boast, or act proudly. It is not self-seeking or easily angered.

It always protects, trusts, hopes, and perseveres.
