# Love and Patience

> True love is patient, kind, and keeps no record of wrongs.

*Station · Hold first (Under fire → Prayer → Truth) · Bedrock · https://bedrock.rippel.ai/c/love-and-patience*

## Under fire

- Love keeps no record of wrongs. Stop replaying their failures.
- Slow the reply. Soften the tone. Love is patient.

## Prayer

Lord, I release the list of wrongs. Soften me. Amen.

## Truth

Love does not envy, boast, or act proudly. It is not self-seeking or easily angered.

It always protects, trusts, hopes, and perseveres.
