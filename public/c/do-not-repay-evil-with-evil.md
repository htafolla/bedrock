# Do Not Repay Evil with Evil

> Do not repay evil with evil or insult with insult.

*Station · Hold first (Under fire → Prayer → Truth) · Bedrock · https://bedrock.rippel.ai/c/do-not-repay-evil-with-evil*

## Under fire

- Bless. Evil repaid multiplies darkness.
- When insult rises, answer with a blessing—or pray one if you cannot speak it yet.

## Prayer

Lord, I release repayment. Teach me to bless. Amen.

## Truth

On the contrary, repay evil with blessing.
