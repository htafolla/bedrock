# Kill the Flesh

> Name the flesh. Three filters. Kill it in three steps. Tools for this hour.

*Station · Hold first (Under fire → Prayer → Truth) · Bedrock · https://bedrock.rippel.ai/c/kill-the-flesh*

## Under fire

- Name it: fear — the flesh. Stop the thought. Capture it. Give it to Christ.
- Three filters: self-less? Protect? Honor God? If no — stop.
- Stop. Breathe. Choose the other path. Do your part. Success is Spirit, not outcomes.

## Prayer

Father, this is fear — this is the flesh. I take it captive to Christ. I refuse control, jealousy, impatience, and rage. Walk me by the Spirit. Amen.

## Truth

Kill the acts of the flesh. Walk in the Spirit. This is the path — not a mood.

[Galatians 5:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A16&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:22–23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A22-23&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:24–25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A24-25&version=NIV)

### 1. Name it

Before you fight, name what is running.

- Surfaces: Thoughts · In-person (words, tone, body, messages).
- Root: Fear.
- Fear produces: Control · Jealousy · Impatience.
- Downstream: Fear → Control / Jealousy / Impatience → Rage → Bitterness · Habit · Immediate relief.

Say it plain: “This is fear. This is the flesh.” Do not dress it up as “just caring,” “just needing to know,” or “just honesty.”

[2 Timothy 1:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Timothy+1%3A7&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:19–21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A19-21&version=NIV) · [James 1:19–20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+1%3A19-20&version=NIV)

### 2. Three filters

Run the act (or the urge) through three questions. All three must pass.

- Is it self-less? (Love does not seek its own way.)
- Does it protect? (Body, soul, family, the weak — not ego, not image.)
- Does it honor God? (Would you do this with Christ in the room?)

If any answer is no → stop. Do not negotiate with the flesh.

[1 Corinthians 13:4–7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+13%3A4-7&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A16&version=NIV) · [Luke 9:23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+9%3A23&version=NIV)

### 3. Kill the flesh (3 steps)

- Name it — “This is fear. This is the flesh.”
- Capture it — Take the thought captive. Give it to Christ. Do not feed it.
- Stop. Breathe. Choose the other path — the next right act in the Spirit, not the old relief.

Prayer: “Father, I take every thought captive. This is flesh. I refuse it. I walk in the Spirit. Amen.”

[2 Corinthians 10:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2+Corinthians+10%3A5&version=NIV) · [Galatians 5:24–25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A24-25&version=NIV) · [Romans 8:13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8%3A13&version=NIV)

### 4. Tools in the moment

When the body is hot and the mind is loud — use steel tools, not more thinking.

- Exhale hard once — empty the panic breath.
- 3-2-1 grounding — 3 things you see, 2 you hear, 1 you feel. Back in the room.
- Default phrase: “My emotional state is my own.” Their choices do not own your peace.

Then return to step 3. Do your part. Their replies are theirs. Success is Spirit, not outcomes.

[James 1:19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=James+1%3A19&version=NIV) · [Proverbs 25:28](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+25%3A28&version=NIV) · [Galatians 6:4–5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+6%3A4-5&version=NIV)
