# Bedrock — Final Product Plan

**Status:** Locked direction for build  
**Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Product:** Bedrock — A Hitchhiker’s Guide to Love · Living · Enduring  
**Motto:** Do Better. Be Better. Trust God.

---

## 1. Why this exists

The author is in the storm — separated from his wife, who is his world. The Lord is using this as a **crucible** not only for personal refining but so others can be helped.

**Public gift (never a crisis headline):**  
Truth, brain hacks, and short prayers so people can **max-cope and grow out of darkness**.

**Sealed (optional):** personal poem / backstory — not marketing.

---

## 2. North star

| | |
|--|--|
| **For** | People in fire — grief, loops, regret, abandonment, marriage fracture |

## 3. Two layers (the big fix)

### Layer A — Problem entry (triage)

People arrive asking: *What’s wrong? What went wrong? How do I move?*

| Storm set | Description | Primary doors |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Grief** | Real + possible loss (spouse, home, “the life we had,” beloveds) | Loss, Lament, Hope |
| **Rumination** | Looping thoughts (where are they, who with, feeds, “already gone”) | Rumination → Thought captive, Renew, Fear |
| **Regret / self-blame** | “I was a moron,” the night that started this | Regret → Fall, Confess, Grace, Cross |
| **Fear of abandonment** | Underneath grief and loops | Fear, Do Not Fear, He is for you, Promises |
| **Out of control** | **Core failure** — can’t force outcome | Wait, Trust |
| **Relational** | Marriage / covenant fracture | Marriage, God on marriage, Forgive, Hatred/vengeance |

### Layer B — Solution atlas (what we already built well)

God First · Cross · Grace · Spirit · Covenant · Armor · Hope · Forgive · etc.

**Keys = Layer A first.**  
**Map / Contents / spine = Layer A + B full web.**

---

## 4. Aggressor and victim — **separate spines**

Do **not** pack both sides into one chamber. They are different altogether.

| Path | Primary doors | Job |
|------|----------------|-----|
| **I blew it** (aggressor / repentance) | Regret · I fell · Confess · Cross · Grace | Own sin → confess → forsake → rise; no self-hate as god |
| **I was hurt** (wounded) | Wounded · Leave vengeance · Do not repay · Forgive · He is for you | Grieve harm; not all of it is your fault; no vengeance; wise bounds; worth in God |
| **Shared storm** (not dual-labeled) | Grief · Loops · Fear · Wait · Trust | Loss, rumination, fear, out-of-control — one path each; no “if aggressor / if victim” in the same Truth or Under fire |

**Never:** excuse abuse, spiritualize away safety, or erase real sin.

---

## 5. Form factor (non-negotiable)

Every chamber / vertex:

1. **Title + one-line summary**  
2. **Truth** — 3–6 short lines  
3. **Under fire** — 1–3 scripture-under-pressure lines (max 3)  
4. **Prayer** — one short prayer  
5. **Scripture** — few Bible Gateway links  
6. **Related** — web edges  

No long essays. No nested comment UI. No form change for fruit or problem doors.

## 8. Success metrics (qualitative)

Someone in the storm can:

1. Name **grief / loops / regret / out of control** in under 10 seconds (Keys).  
2. Get **one hack + one prayer** in under 2 minutes.  
3. See **both** “I caused harm” and “I was harmed” without shame-or-blame only.  
4. Leave with **do better** (action) + **be better** (character) + **trust God** (release).  

---

## 9. What we will not do

- Crisis personal headlines as brand  
- Open comment feeds under Scripture  
- New UI genres (long quiz, therapy app chrome)  
- Control as duplicate chamber (covered by Wait + Trust)  
- Spectacle 3D that blocks mobile usefulness

## 10. One-sentence product

> **Bedrock is a hitchhiker’s field kit for the storm: problem doors into bit-sized truth, hacks, and prayer — so the broken can max-cope, grow out of darkness, and learn patience and trust when everything is out of control.**
