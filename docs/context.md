# Bedrock — Full Context (SSOT)

**Updated:** 2026-07-24  
**Final product name:** Bedrock  
**Original working title:** The Hitchhiker’s Guild · Love · Living · Enduring

## Overall goal

Bedrock is a **field guide to Love · Living · Enduring**.

It defines the core first principles needed to navigate a troubled life — for people who struggle to hold the Word of God in the heat of the moment.

It is meant to be:

- Full of **truth** (Scripture-rooted first principles)
- Full of **usable brain hacks** (short, steel, pressure-ready reframes)
- Full of **short prayers** (words when you cannot find your own)
- A **navigable web**, not only a linear scroll
- Permanent (IPFS + on-chain), so the testimony is hard to erase

**Why “Bedrock”:** these are first principles that remain true when the ground is shaking.

Internal framing (author): *first principles to “hack the matrix” of flesh, fear, and false narrative — by Spirit, not gimmick.* Public tone stays steel and restrained.

## What it is

A personal, curated set of biblical first principles.

A **working field manual** for enduring suffering, walking by the Spirit, and standing firm when everything is on fire.

### Core opening lines

> I hold these things to be true. A lifetime to master them.  
> Though I fall, I get back up. Out of the fire a crucible emerges.

## Origin & purpose

Forged under real pressure — not theory. Written by a disciple of Jesus Christ: a fallen man who works hard to do good and be good, and still falls. The return path: Spirit over flesh, rise after fall, truth over self-deception.

**Product identity:** a Hitchhiker’s field guide to Love · Living · Enduring — first principles for the road. Private fire may have forged it; the public gift is the map, not the headline.

A response to:

> What remains true when the ground is shaking?

## Key theological emphases

- Walking by the Spirit vs. the flesh (Galatians 5)
- Fruit of the Spirit as anti-pattern to rage, impatience, control, negativity, name-calling
- Marriage as lifelong covenant (strong stance against easy divorce)
- Suffering → perseverance → character → hope
- Waiting on the Lord, being still, not fretting
- Forgiveness as non-negotiable
- The righteous fall — and rise again (not pretend sinlessness)
- Dual reality of present pain and future glory / crowns
